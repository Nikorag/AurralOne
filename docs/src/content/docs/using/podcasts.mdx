title: Podcasts
description: Follow podcasts, keep episodes on your server, and pick up where you left off on any device.
---

Podcasts have their own section in the sidebar. Search for a show, or paste its RSS feed URL to add it directly. Follow it to add it to **Following**.

## Keeping episodes

Each show you follow has its own download settings, under **Download settings** on the show's page:

- **Download new episodes automatically**, or only when you ask.
- **Keep the latest**, how many recent episodes stay on the server for you.
- **Remove played episodes** from your downloads. Your progress is kept.

Episodes are stored once, however many people follow the show. Unfollowing releases only your copies, and keeps your listening progress unless you choose to erase it.

## Listening

Playback speed, the skip intervals, the queue, and a sleep timer (a set time, or the end of the episode) are in the player. Open **Now playing** to reach them. Your position saves as you listen, so another device resumes where you stopped. Podcasts and music never play at once: starting one pauses the other.

Episodes that aren't downloaded stream through your server, so they play on any connection and the publisher never sees the listener's address.

**Voice boost** evens out loudness and adds clarity, and **Trim silence** speeds through long pauses. Both are in **Now playing**, and apply to every episode.

If the feed provides chapters, they show as marks on the seek bar, and you can jump to one from **Now playing**.

## Moving and sharing

**Import OPML** and **Export OPML** on the **Following** page move your list to or from another podcast app. Imported shows are followed as they finish, and any feed that can't be added is listed with the reason.

The **Share** button on a show copies its RSS feed URL, which works in any podcast app, or a link that opens the show for other people on your server. An episode's menu copies a link to that episode.

Turn on **Notify me about new episodes** in a show's download settings to get them in your Inbox. Webhooks can listen for the **New podcast episode** event.

## For admins

**Settings > Podcasts** sets the storage limit and shows what is using it, the download queue, failed downloads, and feeds that couldn't be read. When the limit is full, new downloads wait instead of removing episodes people keep. Podcast Index search and trending need a free key from podcastindex.org. Adding a show by RSS URL needs nothing.
